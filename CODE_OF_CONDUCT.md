# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful place for users and contributors. Participation must be free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and unwanted disclosure of private information.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful in issues, discussions, and pull requests.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect privacy: redact account data, credentials, cookies, tokens, and personally identifying information.
- Respect service terms, copyright, and local law when discussing features or testing behaviour.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, hate speech, intimidation, or repeated disruptive conduct.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, deceptive artifacts, or credentials.
- Using project spaces to promote illegal activity or evade safety controls.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that do not meet these expectations. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through an existing maintainer contact channel. For vulnerabilities, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
